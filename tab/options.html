Colours

    Quick sites

    Up to 12 big tiles. Each gets a wireframe sketch and an icon: choose a built-in sketch or upload your own SVG, and use the site's favicon or your own image.

    Categories

    Categories follow the folders on your favourites bar, in the same order. Choose a glyph or upload an icon for each.

    Display

    Browser theme

    An extension cannot recolour the browser frame by itself, so LovedgeLab builds a matching theme from your colours.

    1. Unzip the download.
    2. Open edge://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
    3. Choose Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.

    Sync and backup

    Colours, sites, wireframes and icons sync through your Microsoft account when Edge sync is on for extensions. Notes stay on this device.
