# LovedgeLab privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-06

LovedgeLab is a browser extension that replaces the new tab page. It does not collect, transmit or sell personal data. It has no server, no account, no analytics and no advertising, and it loads no code from the internet.

## What the extension stores

| Data | Where | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Colours, quick sites, wireframe sketches, custom icons, display options | Your browser's extension storage. When browser sync is on for extensions, the browser syncs it between your own devices through your Microsoft account. | So your new tab looks the same everywhere. |
| Notepad text | Local extension storage on this device only. It is never synced. | So your notes are there next time. |
| A copy of the last rendered page | The extension page's local storage on this device. | So the new tab opens instantly. |

Images you upload are shrunk and stored inside the extension's own storage. They are never uploaded to any LovedgeLab server, because none exists.

## Permissions

- **bookmarks** (read only): to show the folders on your favourites bar as category tiles. Bookmark contents are never copied anywhere outside your browser.
- **favicon**: to show site icons from your browser's own favicon cache.
- **storage**: to save the settings described above.

## Sharing

LovedgeLab shares nothing with anyone. Syncing between your devices is performed by your browser vendor under its own privacy policy. When you open a link from the new tab, the destination site receives an ordinary request from your browser, just as if you had typed the address.

## Children

LovedgeLab is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
